Privacy Policy for Wuji Translate

Effective date: November 21, 2026
Last updated: November 21, 2026

Wuji Translate (the “Extension,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Extension handles user data.

1. No Collection of Personally Identifiable Information

The Extension does not operate a developer-owned backend server. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your name, email address, phone number, account information, or browsing history.

Your API keys, translation content, glossaries, and local translation cache are not sent to any server operated by Wuji Translate.

2. Local Storage

The following data is stored only in your current browser’s local extension storage and is not automatically synchronized to other devices:

  • API keys that you provide for third-party AI services
  • Model, translation language, display mode, website rules, and other settings
  • Local glossaries
  • Locally cached translations
  • Keyboard shortcuts and other preferences

You may remove the Extension or clear relevant local data through the Extension settings at any time.

3. Third-Party AI Services

When you actively use webpage translation, selection translation, paragraph translation, or input translation, the Extension sends the content necessary to complete the translation directly from your browser to the third-party AI service API that you configure.

This content may include text to be translated, the target language, necessary context, matched glossary terms, and structural information required to preserve webpage formatting. These requests do not pass through servers operated by Wuji Translate.

You may choose and configure your own AI service provider or a custom service compatible with the OpenAI API. Your use of those services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

Do not translate passwords, identification numbers, payment card details, trade secrets, or other sensitive information unless you understand and accept the data practices of the AI service provider you selected.

4. Pronunciation Services

When you actively select the pronunciation feature for an English word or phrase, the relevant text may be sent via HTTPS to Youdao Dictionary’s pronunciation service to retrieve audio. Youdao may process the request and ordinary network connection information, such as your IP address, according to its own policies.

For other languages, sentences, or paragraphs, the Extension may use the Web Speech API provided by your browser or operating system. The actual processing of voice data is governed by the browser, operating system, and applicable service providers.

5. Data Security

API keys are stored locally in the Extension’s private browser storage and are accessed only when needed by the Extension settings and background translation tasks. The Extension does not intentionally include your complete API key in logs, error messages, screenshots, or ordinary settings exports.

However, browser local storage is not an encrypted vault. Please secure your device and browser account, and review or rotate your API keys regularly.

6. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices materially change, we will update the “Last updated” date above and publish the latest version on our website.
